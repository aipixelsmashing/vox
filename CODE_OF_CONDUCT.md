# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be decent. Assume good faith, critique the work rather than the person, and accept
that maintainers may say no to your idea without that being a personal judgement.

Report unacceptable behaviour to `aipixelsmashing@gmail.com`. Reports are handled privately by
the maintainers, who will respond within a week.
